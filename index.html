Before you sign
Things to know
Term & timing

Your membership can start as early as fall 2026. Terms run as short as three months, and every membership ends on or before October 2027 — built for a defined project or season, not a long-term home.

What's in your membership

Your monthly membership covers your space plus shared power, water, internet, corridors, restrooms and utility rooms — and call routing so your business line rings straight through to you. You carry your own business insurance.

Best-fit uses

Dry storage, e-commerce fulfillment, light assembly and distribution. Food-grade storage requires Washington WSDA warehouse licensing (RCW 69.10) — budget a few weeks to process, and we can point you to the steps.

Shared loading

Grade-level and dock-high doors, plus shared carts and pallet jacks. Dock windows are coordinated so every member gets reliable access.

Delivered as-is

Spaces come broom-clean. Light improvements like partitions or shelving can be arranged.

Becoming a member

A deposit and first month's membership fee reserve your space. Membership is personal and non-transferable, and all space is subject to master-landlord consent.
